A short documentary following a single day inside Markthalle Neun, a historic market hall in Berlin-Kreuzberg.

view project →

01 — the concept

The Concept

The idea was to let the market speak for itself — no narrator, no interviews explaining what we're looking at, just the rhythm of a working market from open to close: stallholders setting up, the midday rush, the quiet teardown.

02 — the process

The Process

Pre-production was mostly about earning access and trust — several visits before we ever brought a camera. Production was observational, shooting long and quiet to catch unscripted moments. Post was where the film actually got built: cutting hours of footage down to a single day's arc.

on location
color grading

03 — the result

The Result

The finished piece leans on colour grading and sound design to carry mood in place of narration — warmer tones for the morning rush, cooler and quieter for teardown.

04 — reflection

Reflection

Observational documentary is unforgiving of a weak structure — without an interview or voiceover to lean on, the whole film lives or dies in the edit. That's where I learned the most: how much a cut can imply without a single word of explanation.
