shuffle

mobile music & workout app

shuffle

design

A music and fitness app with customisation and social features.

overview

context: intern project

timeline: 18 weeks

role: UX research, UI design, prototyping

tools: Figma

01 — the problem

The Problem

Competitor research showed most fitness apps treat music as an afterthought (a Spotify link, at best), while most music apps ignore activity entirely. The goal: a single app where the music and the workout inform each other, without duplicating either category's whole feature set.

02 — the process

The Process

Started with 3 personas and journey maps from short user interviews, then moved into a competitor teardown of 5 existing apps to find the actual gap. Information architecture and a design system came next, before touching high-fidelity screens.

persona board
IA sketch

03 — the solution

The Solution

An interactive, functional prototype in Figma — home screen surfaces "what to play" based on workout type, with lightweight social features (sharing a playlist + run) layered on top rather than centred.

04 — reflection

Reflection & Takeaways

The competitor analysis ended up being the most valuable step — it's what turned a vague "music + fitness app" brief into a specific, defensible design decision. Would start every project with it now.
